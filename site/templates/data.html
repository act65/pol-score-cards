The scores are derived from two sources for the 54th New Zealand Parliament: the Hansard debate record (every MP), and the transcripts of the weekly post-Cabinet press conferences (the PM and senior ministers). Statements from both are blended into each politician's scores; the breakdown below shows exactly how much data sits behind the cards, and where it comes from.

Hansard covers every MP; the pressers add the PM and senior ministers' unscripted public statements, blended into the same scores. Because pressers are government-only, they concentrate on frontbenchers — so a minister's score draws on both arenas while a backbencher's is Hansard alone. Scoring each arena separately (to show a debate-vs-public delta) is planned for a later version.
