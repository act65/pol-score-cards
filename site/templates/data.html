The scores are derived from three sources for the 54th New Zealand Parliament: the Hansard debate record (every MP), the transcripts of the weekly post-Cabinet press conferences (the PM and senior ministers), and the parties' press releases. Statements from all three are blended into each politician's scores; the breakdown below shows exactly how much data sits behind the cards, and where it comes from.

Overall scores by party

Each MP's overall score is the geometric mean of their attributes (0–100) — the same measure the cards rank by, where one weak attribute drags the whole score down. Party means sit close together; what differs is the spread inside each caucus. That now has its own page, built from the live dataset rather than a precomputed image, with a full aggregated scorecard per party: party scorecards →
