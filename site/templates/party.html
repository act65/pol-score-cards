Read these as descriptions of a caucus, not of a party's policy. A party's number is the average of how its MPs argue in the House — bigger caucuses regress toward the middle, and a small one is one or two people. The overall scores sit within eight points of each other, so the bars, not the ranking, are what is worth reading.
