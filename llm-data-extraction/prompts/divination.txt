You are an expert at identifying predictions made by politicians about future events. Your task is to read the following text and extract all statements that forecast or predict what will happen in the future.

For each prediction, output a JSON object in JSONL format with the following keys:

- "politician": The name of the politician making the prediction.
- "prediction": The text of the prediction. (You are allowed to rephrase the prediction to make it more clear and concise and to resolve any ambiguities like 'it', 'this' or 'that'. Also, remember to include any required context to parse the prediction.)
- "date_made": The date the prediction was made (DD-MM-YYYY format if available, otherwise infer as best as possible or leave as null).
- "resolution_date": The date or timeframe when the prediction is expected to be resolved or the predicted event is expected to occur (DD-MM-YYYY format). If the date is not explicit / uncertain, pick a resonable upper bound (e.g., end of the year, end of the term).
- "importance_weighting": A score between 1 (low importance) and 5 (high importance) indicating the perceived significance or impact of the prediction. Consider factors like the scope of the prediction (local, national, international), the number of people it might affect, and the potential consequences if the prediction comes true.
- "certainty": A score between 0.0 and 1.0 indicating your certainty that this statement is intended as a prediction about the future.

Notes:

- When uncertain about the date the prediction was made, use the date of the article or the date of the event being reported on.
- We are only interested in politically relevant predictions related to future events and consequences of actions now, not what a politician says they will do.
- Only consider predictions that are verifiable or falsifiable. If a prediction is too vague or ambiguous to be verified, it should not be included.
- If multiple predictions are made by the same politician in one phrase, output one object per prediction.

Examples:

Text:
Speaking at a rally on January 15, 2024, Prime Minister Smith said, "Our government's policies will lead to unemployment falling below 4% by the end of this year."

Output:
{"politician": "Prime Minister Smith", "prediction": "Our government's policies will lead to unemployment falling below 4% by the end of this year.", "date_made": "15-01-2024", "resolution_date": "31-12-2024", "importance_weighting": 4, "certainty": 0.95}
