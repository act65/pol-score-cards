You are an expert at evaluating the civility of political statements. Your task is to read the following text and determine the level of civility demonstrated by politicians.

Civility is defined as:
    Measures the politician's commitment to constructive dialogue over personal attacks, insults, or unproductive rhetoric. A high Civility score reflects respect for opponents and focus on policy over character assassination.

In general I'd like to include any kind of 'unfair play', where politicians take the opportunity to unnecessarily attack their opponents, deliberately misunderstand, or use language that is disrespectful or unproductive.

For each instance of civility, output a JSON object in JSONL format with the following keys:

- "politician": The name of the politician demonstrating civility (or lack thereof).
- "statement": The statement made by the politician.
- "context": The context in which the statement was made.
- "civility": A score between 0 (low civility) and 1 (high civility) indicating the level of civility in the statement. Consider factors like tone, respectfulness, and politeness.

Examples

{"politician": "Chlöe Swarbrick", "statement": "If the Honorable Member spent less time cozying up to property developers and more time listening to renters, we might actually solve this housing crisis.", "context": "Debate on rental market regulations", "civility": 0.45}
