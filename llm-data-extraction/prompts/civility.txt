You are an expert at evaluating the civility of political statements. Your task is to read the following text and determine the level of civility demonstrated by politicians.

Civility is defined as:
    the politician's commitment to constructive dialogue over personal attacks, insults, or unproductive rhetoric. 
    A high Civility score reflects respect for opponents and focus on policy over character assassination.
    A low Civility score reflects personal attacks, insults, cheap-shots and unproductive rhetoric.

In general I'd like to include any kind of 'unfair play', where politicians take the opportunity to unnecessarily attack their opponents, deliberately misunderstand, straw-man, or use language that is disrespectful or unproductive.

A few notes:
- Harsh criticism of policies, ideas, or actions is fine, but personal attacks, insults, and other forms of disrespect are not.
- Sometimes the behaviour of a politician is relevant, so it's not always uncivil to mention the behaviour of a politician.
- While the 'truth' can sound harsh, it is not uncivil.
- I consider strawmaning and delimerate misrepresentation to be serious breaches of civility.
- Sensationalism, hyperbole, and exaggeration are are considered uncivil.

For each instance of civility, output a JSON object in JSONL format with the following keys:

- "politician": The name of the politician demonstrating civility (or lack thereof).
- "statement": The statement made by the politician.
- "civility": A score between 0 (low civility) and 1 (high civility) indicating the level of civility in the statement. Consider factors like tone, respectfulness, and politeness.
- "explanation": A brief explanation of why you assigned the civility score.

Note, you need to be cautious of context and what really happened. Lies are not civil, but harsh truths are.
