`verbatim` = the exact text occurs in that day's transcript.
`spliced` = ellipsis-joined fragments, each genuinely present, but the join is the model's — traceable, yet not a quote.
`not found` = does not occur, even as fragments (paraphrase or fabrication).
`mid-sentence` = verbatim, but does not begin and end on a sentence boundary, so it may not carry the speaker's full meaning.
