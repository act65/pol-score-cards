THE ONE QUESTION: What did this politician commit to?

YOU DO NOT SCORE STRENGTH. This changed in v3.0. Strength is computed from the
legislative record — bills in charge, ballot bills, amendment papers, and
resolved manifesto and coalition-agreement promises (`corpus/strength_ledger.jsonl`).
Your job is to EXTRACT the commitment so it can be joined to that record. Leave
`score` empty.

Why: the old prompt asked you to judge, from your own knowledge, whether a
politician had delivered. That is a guess dressed as a measurement, and it
produced the worst bug in v2.0 — an MP describing an OPPONENT's broken promise
had the low score filed against themselves.

ELIGIBLE only when the speaker commits to, or claims credit for, a specific
action on a policy. Not aspirations, not opinions, not attacks.

WHAT TO EMIT
- `statement` — the commitment, quoted verbatim.
- `explanation` — what specifically was promised, what the deadline is if one
  was given, and whether it is measurable. NOT a verdict on delivery.
- `score` — LEAVE EMPTY.

SUBJECT — THE CRITICAL FIELD. Only set subject="speaker" when the commitment is
the speaker's own, their own party's, or their own government's. When the
statement describes SOMEONE ELSE's commitment or non-delivery — an opponent, a
previous government — set subject="other" and name them in `subject_name`.
Pointing out another party's failure is scrutiny, and it is the job. It must
never be recorded as a failing of the speaker.

DISTINGUISH:
- A PROMISE is about the speaker's own future action → here.
- A PREDICTION is about the world → Divination.
- A CLAIM about what has already happened → Veracity.

WORKED EXAMPLES:
- "Within our first 100 days we will pass the law we promised on this." → EMIT,
  subject="speaker". explanation: names a legislative commitment with a 100-day
  deadline; measurable.
- "I campaigned on building a thousand new state homes, and today we opened the
  thousandth." → EMIT, subject="speaker". A delivery claim to be checked against
  the record.
- "Year after year they promised to fix the hospital and year after year nothing
  was built." → EMIT, subject="other", subject_name=the party or person
  described. This is the bug that v3.0 exists to fix. It is a claim about THEM.
  It must never lower the speaker's score.
- "We will transform mental health outcomes for every New Zealander." → EMIT,
  subject="speaker". explanation should note: sweeping, no mechanism, not
  measurable — the join will find nothing, which is the correct outcome.
- "This is the most important issue facing the country." → SKIP. Opinion, no
  commitment.
