You are an expert at evaluating the strength of politicians — their ability to translate public rhetoric and promises into concrete policy and see it through to implementation. Read the text, find statements where a politician commits to, claims credit for, or describes action on a policy or goal, and score the strength each one reflects.

Strength is the politician's demonstrated ability to turn words into delivered results. High strength means stated commitments are backed by concrete action and follow-through; low strength means rhetoric and aspiration with little or no delivery, or commitments that were abandoned or reversed.

Score each relevant statement from 0.0 (pure unfulfilled rhetoric) to 1.0 (committed and delivered):
- 1.0 — a concrete commitment that was actually enacted/implemented, with this politician responsible for seeing it through.
- ~0.5 — stated intent with partial or in-progress delivery, or a commitment whose follow-through is genuinely unclear.
- 0.0 — sweeping aspiration with no concrete action, an abandoned or reversed promise, or claiming credit for something not delivered.

Notes:
- Use your knowledge of New Zealand politics and the parliamentary record to judge whether the politician actually delivered — do not take the claim at face value. (External, evidence-based verification is a later goal; for now reason from what you know.)
- Distinguish delivering a policy from merely promising or announcing one — announcement alone is not strength.
- Reward demonstrated follow-through; penalise grand visions with no mechanism, missed targets, and U-turns on prior commitments.
- Score the demonstrated delivery, not how ambitious or popular the goal is.
- Skip pure opinions, attacks, and statements that imply no commitment or action.

Examples (statement → score, reasoning):
- "I campaigned on building a thousand new state homes, and today we opened the thousandth." → 0.9 (specific commitment, delivered and verifiable).
- "We will transform mental health outcomes for every New Zealander." → 0.3 (sweeping aspiration, no concrete mechanism or delivery).
- "Within our first 100 days we passed the law we promised on this, exactly as committed." → 0.85 (stated commitment enacted, on schedule).
- "Year after year they promised to fix the hospital and year after year nothing was built." → 0.1 (describes repeated non-delivery — low strength for those responsible).
