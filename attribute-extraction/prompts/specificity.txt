THE ONE QUESTION: Is there checkable content in the statement?

ELIGIBLE only where the statement is trying to convey substance. Thanks,
procedural courtesies, ceremonial remarks and pleasantries have no content to
assess — SKIP THEM rather than scoring them low.

Specificity fired on 51% of the corpus in the previous version, which is far too
much for an attribute that is supposed to distinguish anything. Be selective.

SCORING
- 1.0 — Concrete and precise: figures, mechanisms, timeframes, or named
  policies. Someone could check it or act on it.
- ~0.5 — A direction with some detail, but missing the how, the how much, or the
  by when.
- 0.0 — A platitude, slogan or ambiguous statement committing to nothing.

Judge in context. "I will fix the economy" is vague in isolation but specific if
the surrounding text lays out the plan — credit the detail the speaker actually
supplied nearby. Do not strip context to make a statement look vaguer than it is.

THIS IS *NOT* SPECIFICITY — do not let these move the score:
- Whether the content is TRUE → Veracity. A precise, checkable, completely false
  claim ("unemployment is 40 percent") scores HIGH here.
- Whether it ANSWERS THE QUESTION → Forthrightness. A blunt, direct "no" is
  fully forthright and highly unspecific.
- Whether the argument is VALID → Rigor.
- What the statement is ABOUT → Focus. Focus asks policy-versus-party;
  Specificity asks how much detail is carried. A statement squarely about a
  policy can still be a platitude.

KNOWN BIAS, and how to handle it. Specificity rewards figures, mechanisms and
named policies — which is what a minister announcing a programme produces, and
what an opposition MP asking why something has not happened does not. Do not
give a statement extra credit merely for being an announcement. A precise
question ("how many of the 1,000 homes are built?") is as specific as a precise
answer.

WORKED BOUNDARY EXAMPLES:
- "We will lift the minimum wage to $25/hour by April 2026." → 0.95 (figure and
  date).
- "Unemployment has risen to 40 percent under this government." → 0.9 on
  Specificity. It is precise and checkable. It is also false — Veracity's
  problem, not this one.
- "We'll invest more in public transport, starting with the regional rail
  business case next year." → 0.6 (named action, rough timing, no amounts).
- "We're committed to supporting hard-working families." → 0.05 (slogan).
- "These steps are consistent with requiring our science system to be
  productive, to be efficient, to be sustainable." → 0.1 (a string of generic
  virtues; no figures, mechanisms or named steps).
- "I thank the member for the question." → SKIP. Not trying to convey content.
